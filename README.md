# Retail Sales Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

The **Retail Sales Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive Microsoft Excel dashboard designed to analyze retail sales performance and identify meaningful business insights. It presents key performance indicators (KPIs), category-wise sales, monthly sales trends, and gender-based sales metrics through charts and interactive slicers.

## Dashboard Preview
![Retail Sales Analysis Dashboard](dashboard.png)

## Project Objectives

- Analyze overall retail sales performance.
- Compare sales across product categories.
- Track monthly sales trends throughout the year.
- Understand sales distribution by gender.
- Monitor transaction-related KPIs.
- Build an interactive dashboard for efficient business reporting.

##  Dashboard Features

### 1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Total Sales
- Average Sales
- Maximum Transactions
- Minimum Transactions
- Total Transactions

### 2. Sales Analysis
- Total Sales by Category
- Quantity Sold by Category
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Sales Share by Gender (%)
- Product Category Metrics by Gender

### 3. Interactive Filters
- Year
- Gender

These filters allow users to explore the dashboard dynamically and compare sales performance across different selections.

Key Business Questions

This dashboard helps answer the following questions:

- What are the total and average sales?
- Which product category generates the highest sales?
- Which category has the highest quantity sold?
- How do sales change from month to month?
- What is the sales distribution between female and male customers?
- How do product category metrics differ by gender?
- How does sales performance vary by year?
